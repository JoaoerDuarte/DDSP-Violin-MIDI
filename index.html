DDSP-Violin MIDI — Audio examples

Listening comparisons of the models in the MIDI studies.

First set: the original DDSP model against DDSP-Violin, trained the same way on the same recordings.

Models

DDSP

The solo-instrument model of Engel et al. (ICLR 2020), run with the published code (magenta/ddsp 3.7.0, solo_instrument.gin).

  • Inputs: pitch and loudness. No Z encoder.
  • Decoder: one 3-layer MLP (512) per input, GRU (512), 3-layer MLP (512).
  • Outputs per frame: amplitude, 60 harmonic amplitudes, 65 noise-band magnitudes.
  • Synthesis: harmonic oscillator bank plus filtered noise.
  • Reverb: trainable, 3 s (48,000 samples), on the sum of harmonics and noise, dry signal added. No body filter.
Diagram

From Engel et al. (2020). The Z encoder (dashed) is not used by the solo-instrument model.

DDSP-Violin

Our physically-informed version (code, EUSIPCO 2026).

  • Inputs: pitch, loudness and brightness α. No Z encoder.
  • Decoder: as DDSP.
  • Outputs per frame: amplitude, 60 per-harmonic residuals (within ±12 dB), 65 noise-band magnitudes.
  • Bow-position mask: open position.
  • Body filter: learned, 2,048 samples, on the harmonic part.
  • DDSP-Violin + room reverb: the same trainable 3 s reverb as DDSP, on the sum of the filtered harmonics and the noise.
Diagram

The Z encoder is not used here: α comes from the recording, β and γ are fixed.

Preprocessing

  • Audio: 16 kHz mono, one gain per dataset.
  • Frame rate: 250 per second (4 ms).
  • Pitch: CREPE (full model, Viterbi decoding). A frame without a tracked pitch takes the pitch of the nearest tracked frame.
  • Loudness: A-weighted power over 512-sample windows, in dB.
  • Brightness α (DDSP-Violin only): fitted per frame to the harmonic amplitudes of the recording.
  • Training examples: 4 s windows cut every second.

The DDSP rows of this version were trained with the library's own extraction of the same features (its CREPE and loudness, raw audio level). The runs on the shared extraction replace them when they finish.

Training

  • Sets: solo violin, all in training, as in the DDSP paper. MOSA, a training set and a test set of full performance takes (training 13.8 h, of which 36 min of ev03). In the results, training set and test set mark where the excerpt comes from.
  • Loss: multi-scale spectral, L1 on the magnitude and on the log magnitude, FFT sizes 2048, 1024, 512, 256, 128, 64.
  • Optimiser: Adam, 3e-4 decaying by 0.98 every 10K steps, gradient clip 3.
  • Batch 32, 50K steps, one model per dataset, as the published DDSP solo-violin checkpoint.
  • Extended trainings: 150K steps on ev03, 300K on all players, in progress.
