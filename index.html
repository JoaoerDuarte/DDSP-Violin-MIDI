DDSP-Violin MIDI — Audio examples

Listening comparisons of the models in the MIDI studies.

First set: the original DDSP model against DDSP-Violin, trained with the same recipe on the same recordings. Every row of a results table is the same 10 s of the same take under each model.

Models

DDSP

The solo-instrument model of Engel et al. (ICLR 2020), run with the published code (magenta/ddsp 3.7.0, solo_instrument.gin): a decoder from pitch and loudness to an amplitude, 60 harmonics and 65 noise bands, a harmonic oscillator bank and filtered noise, then a trainable 3 s reverb that stands for the body and the room.

Diagram

From Engel et al. (2020). The Z encoder (dashed) is not used by the solo-instrument model.

DDSP-Violin

Our physically-informed version (code, EUSIPCO 2026): the same decoder and synthesis, with the harmonic levels constrained by a brightness tilt (α, measured on the recording and given as a third input), the bow-position mask (here the open position) and per-harmonic residuals within ±12 dB, followed by a learned body filter (2048 taps). DDSP-Violin + room reverb adds, after the body filter, the same trainable 3 s reverb as DDSP.

Diagram

The Z encoder is not used here: α comes from the recording, β and γ are fixed.

Splits. The solo violin set is all training, as in the DDSP paper. MOSA is split by take into a training set (13.8 h; 36 min of ev03) and a test set of whole takes (the ev03 model sees only the ev03 takes of each). In the results, training set and test set mark where the excerpt comes from.

Preprocessing

  • Audio at 16 kHz mono, one gain per dataset; 4 s training examples cut every second.
  • Pitch: CREPE (full model, Viterbi decoding), 250 frames per second; untracked frames filled from the nearest tracked frame.
  • Loudness: A-weighted power over 512-sample windows, in dB.
  • DDSP-Violin also receives the brightness α measured per frame on the harmonic amplitudes.

The DDSP rows of this version were trained with the library's own extraction of the same features (its CREPE and loudness, raw audio level); the runs on the shared extraction replace them when they finish.

Training

Both models with the recipe of the published DDSP solo-violin checkpoint: multi-scale spectral loss (L1 on the magnitude and the log magnitude at FFT sizes 2048 to 64), Adam at 3e-4 decaying by 0.98 every 10K steps, batch 32, 50K steps. One model per dataset. Extended trainings (150K steps on ev03, 300K on all players) are in progress and will be added.
