DDSP-Violin MIDI — Audio examples

Listening comparisons of the models in the MIDI studies.

First set: the original DDSP model against DDSP-Violin, trained with the same recipe on the same recordings. Every row of a results table is the same 5 s of the same take under each model. training marks an excerpt the models were trained on (the condition of the original DDSP examples), held out one they never saw.

Models

DDSP

The solo-instrument model of Engel et al. (ICLR 2020), run with the published code (magenta/ddsp 3.7.0, solo_instrument.gin): a decoder from pitch and loudness to an amplitude, 60 harmonics and 65 noise bands, a harmonic oscillator bank and filtered noise, then a trainable 3 s reverb that stands for the body and the room. Loss: multi-scale spectral (six FFT sizes); Adam 3e-4 decaying 0.98 per 10K steps, batch 32, 50K steps, as the published checkpoint.

Diagram

From Engel et al. (2020). The Z encoder (dashed) is not used by the solo-instrument model.

DDSP-Violin

Our physically-informed version (code, EUSIPCO 2026): the same decoder and synthesis, with the harmonic levels constrained by a brightness tilt (α, measured on the recording and given as a third input), the bow-position mask (here the open position) and per-harmonic residuals within ±12 dB, followed by a learned body filter (2048 taps). Trained with the same loss, optimiser, batch size and steps as DDSP. DDSP-Violin + room reverb adds, after the body filter, the same trainable 3 s reverb as DDSP.

Diagram

The Z encoder is not used here: α comes from the recording, β and γ are fixed.

Preprocessing

One extraction for both models:

  • Audio at 16 kHz mono, one gain per dataset; 4 s training examples cut every second.
  • Pitch: CREPE (full model, Viterbi decoding), 250 frames per second; untracked frames filled from the nearest tracked frame.
  • Loudness: A-weighted power over 512-sample windows, in dB.
  • DDSP-Violin also receives the brightness α measured per frame on the harmonic amplitudes.

The DDSP rows of this version were trained with the library's own extraction of the same features (its CREPE and loudness, raw audio level); the runs on the shared extraction replace them when they finish.
