DDSP-Violin MIDI — Audio Examples

Companion page of the MIDI-controllable violin synthesiser (in preparation). First set: the original DDSP solo-instrument model and our model under the same recipe.

Every model synthesises the excerpt from its pitch and loudness curves (CREPE pitch, A-weighted loudness, 250 frames per second). Columns:

  • DDSP, Garner / ev03 — the original DDSP solo-instrument model (Engel et al., 2020; magenta/ddsp 3.7.0, published recipe, 50K steps), trained on the paper's 13-minute solo violin set (John Garner, Bach BWV 1002) or on one MOSA player (ev03, 36 minutes).
  • Ours — DDSP-Violin (brightness and bow-position masks, residuals, body filter), trained with the same recipe on the same data; room adds the 3 s learned reverb of the original model; full MOSA is the 14-hour training split of 15 players.
