Setup

AimTo hear each recording taken apart into harmonics and noise, and put back by the synthesiser when its parameters are the measured ones. Nothing in the first two parts comes from a trained model.
ExcerptsThe long-note excerpt of each dataset's training and test sets, as on the synthesiser's page.
Harmonics, multiples of f0The sinusoids that sinusoidal modelling (SMS) takes at the peaks near the multiples of the pitch, each at its measured frequency.
Harmonics, all peaksThose plus every other spectral peak. The other peaks are mostly earlier notes still sounding.
Noise componentThe recording minus all peaks, with nothing else done to it.
The two sumsAll peaks + noise is the recording again, by construction. Multiples of f0 + noise is the recording without the other peaks.
Harmonic synthesiserThe synthesiser's oscillators at exact multiples of the pitch curve, each harmonic at the level SMS measured for it every 4 ms.
Noise synthesiserThe synthesiser's filtered noise (65 bands) at the band levels of the noise component every 4 ms.
